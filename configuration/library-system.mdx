title: "Manage books, authors, and borrow records in the Library"
description: "Use the Library System module to catalog books and research papers, group them by category and author, and track who has borrowed each title from the admin panel."
---

The Library System module turns KoAkademy into a lightweight integrated library catalog. Administrators can register books and research papers, organize them with categories and authors, and track every borrow and return from one place.

## When to use this module

Enable Library System if your institution runs a campus library, a department reading room, or any collection where you need to know which titles you own and who currently has them.

## What you can do

- Maintain a **Books** catalog with cover image, ISBN, edition, and stock counts.
- Maintain an **Authors** directory and link each book to its authors.
- Organize the catalog with **Categories** for easy browsing and reporting.
- Record **Borrow records** that tie a book to a borrower, with due dates and return status.
- Track **Research papers** with file uploads and associated students.

## Catalog structure

| Resource | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Books | Each title in your collection, with stock and metadata |
| Authors | People credited with writing books and research papers |
| Categories | Subject groupings used to filter and browse the catalog |
| Borrow records | Active and historical loans, including due dates |
| Research papers | Student-authored or institutional papers with attachments |

## Enable Library System

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin panel">
    Sign in as an administrator and go to **Modules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the module">
    Toggle **Library System** on. The change applies immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your taxonomy">
    Visit **Library → Authors** and **Library → Categories** to create the entities you will reference when adding books.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the books list">
    Go to **Library → Books** and select **New book**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the catalog fields">
    Enter the title, ISBN, edition, publisher, and stock count. Upload a cover image if available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign authors and category">
    Pick one or more authors from the directory and choose a category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The book is now searchable in the catalog and available for borrow records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record a borrow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Borrow records">
    Go to **Library → Borrow records** and select **New borrow record**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the book and borrower">
    Choose the book, the borrower, and the due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as returned">
    When the book is returned, open the record and set its status to **Returned**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage research papers

Research papers behave like books with attached files. Use this for thesis archives, capstone projects, or institutional research. Each paper can list its authors, a category, and the students who contributed.
