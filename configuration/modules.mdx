---
title: "Enabling and disabling feature modules"
description: "KoAkademy ships with optional feature modules — Announcements, Cashier, Library, and more — that you can toggle on or off to match your institution's needs."
---

KoAkademy is built around a modular architecture. Core features like enrollment, grades, and finances are always available, but several extended capabilities are packaged as optional modules. You can enable only the modules your institution uses, keeping the portal focused and uncluttered for your users.

## Available modules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Announcements" icon="bullhorn">
    Broadcast messages to students and faculty through the portal. Admins can target announcements to specific groups or make them visible to everyone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cashier" icon="cash-register">
    Extended billing and payment processing workflows. Enables cashier staff to record and reconcile payments beyond the standard tuition ledger.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inventory" icon="boxes-stacked">
    Track physical assets and resources such as rooms and equipment. Useful for institutions that manage room reservations or equipment loans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Library System" icon="book">
    Book catalog, borrowing, and return tracking. Students and faculty can search the catalog and view their current loans through the portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Notification Center" icon="bell">
    In-app and push notifications for events and deadlines. Students receive reminders about enrollment windows, grade postings, and payment due dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Student Medical Records" icon="heart-pulse">
    Health records and medical clearance tracking. Supports clinic staff in managing student health information and issuing medical clearances.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to enable or disable a module

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the admin panel">
    Log in to the admin panel and navigate to **Modules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the module list">
    Each module is listed with its current status. A toggle or status indicator shows whether it is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the module">
    Click the toggle next to the module you want to enable or disable. The change is saved immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you toggle a module in the admin panel, the change takes effect immediately for all users — no additional configuration is needed.

## What happens when you disable a module

Disabling a module hides it from all portal users immediately. Students and faculty will no longer see the module's navigation items or pages. No data is deleted.

<Warning>
  Disabling a module removes access for all users at once. Communicate changes to your users before disabling a module they actively use.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Re-enabling a module after it has been disabled restores access to all existing data. Nothing is lost during the period the module is inactive.
</Note>
