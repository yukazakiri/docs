KoAkademy is a modern academic management platform built for schools and universities. It brings enrollment, academics, finances, and faculty tools into a single portal so your institution does not need to switch between separate systems. This page introduces what KoAkademy offers, who it is designed for, and how to find what you need across the documentation.

## Key features

KoAkademy covers every stage of the academic lifecycle, from a student's first application through graduation, and gives administrators the tools to configure and manage the platform for their institution.

## Who uses KoAkademy

KoAkademy serves three groups of users. Your experience and available features depend on the role assigned to your account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Students">
    As a student, you use the student portal to manage every part of your academic life. You can submit your enrollment request each term, view your class schedule, check posted grades, monitor your tuition balance, and download your Statement of Account. You also have access to a digital ID card and receive announcements from your institution.

    Your account follows the student lifecycle — you start as an applicant, become enrolled, and eventually graduate or are marked as withdrawn or dropped depending on your status.

    **Get started:** [Student portal dashboard](/student-portal/dashboard)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Faculty">
    As a faculty member, you use the faculty portal to manage your classes. You can view your assigned class lists, take attendance, post grades at the end of a term, and communicate important updates to your students.

    **Get started:** [Faculty portal overview](/faculty-portal/overview)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Administrators">
    As an administrator, you have access to the full admin panel. You can manage student records, process enrollment requests, configure the school year and semester settings, customize branding and modules, and generate reports. You can also create and manage accounts for students and faculty.

    **Get started:** [Configuration overview](/configuration/branding)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Platform availability

KoAkademy is a web-based platform. You can access it from any modern browser — no software installation is required.

- **Student portal:** `portal.koakademy.edu` (your institution may use a custom subdomain)
- **Admin panel:** `admin.koakademy.edu` (for administrators)

KoAkademy also supports installation as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** on mobile devices and desktops. Installing the PWA gives you a dedicated app icon, faster load times, and an experience that feels like a native application.

<Tip>
  On mobile, open the portal in your browser and select "Add to Home Screen" (iOS Safari) or "Install app" (Android Chrome) to install the PWA.
</Tip>

## Supported academic structures

KoAkademy is built to accommodate different academic program types common in Philippine education:

- **College programs** — four-year and post-secondary degree programs with semester or trimester terms
- **Senior High School (SHS)** — strands and tracks under the K–12 curriculum, including Academic, TVL, Sports, and Arts & Design strands

Academic year and semester settings are managed by your institution's administrator. If you notice your current term is incorrect, contact your registrar or see [Academic settings](/configuration/academic-settings).
