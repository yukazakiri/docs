The faculty portal is your central workspace in KoAkademy. From here you can manage every aspect of your teaching responsibilities — viewing your assigned classes, entering and submitting grades, recording attendance, posting assignments, and reviewing student submissions — all without leaving your browser. Before you can access the portal, your account must be verified by an administrator.

## What the faculty portal provides

The portal brings together the tools you need across the full course lifecycle:

  </Card>
  <Card title="Grade entry" icon="pen-to-square">

  <Card title="Attendance tracking" icon="clipboard-check">

  <Card title="Student information" icon="user">

  <Card title="Class posts" icon="bullhorn">

## Navigating the portal

After logging in and passing faculty verification, you land on your dashboard. The main navigation includes:

| Section | Path | What you'll find |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard | `/faculty/dashboard` | Summary of upcoming classes, pending actions, and recent activity |
| Classes | `/faculty/classes` | Your assigned classes for the current term |
| Schedule | `/faculty/schedule` | Weekly view of your class meeting times |
| Action center | `/faculty/action-center` | All pending items requiring your attention |
| Announcements | — | System-wide and institution announcements |
| Profile | `/faculty/profile` | Your personal and contact information |

## Action center

The action center at `/faculty/action-center` collects every item that needs your attention in one place. This includes grades waiting to be submitted, student submissions ready to review, and any administrative tasks assigned to you. The status of each activity can be updated directly from this page, keeping your pending list current.

## Switching semesters and school years

Your portal displays data for the active semester by default. To view a different term, go to your faculty settings and select the semester or school year you want. Classes, grades, and attendance records update to reflect the selected period.

## Digital ID card

Your faculty ID card is available at `/faculty/id-card`. It displays your name, photo, department, and faculty ID number. You can present it digitally or print it when required for on-campus access or identification.

<Note>
  Your account must be verified by an administrator before you can access any part of the faculty portal. If you see a verification prompt after logging in, contact your registrar or department coordinator to complete the process.
</Note>
