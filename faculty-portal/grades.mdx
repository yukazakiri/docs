---
title: 'Recording and submitting student grades'
description: 'Enter prelim, midterm, and final grades for each student in your classes, then submit them for administrator review and finalization.'
---

Grades in KoAkademy are organized by grading period — prelim, midterm, and finals — and are entered directly from the class detail page. Once you have recorded scores for a period, you submit them to trigger the review workflow. Submitted grades go to your administrator for verification before they become official and visible to students.

## Opening the grades tab

Navigate to `/faculty/classes` and select the class you want to grade. On the class detail page, open the **Grades** tab. You will see a list of enrolled students with columns for each grading period.

## Entering grades

Grades are entered as numeric values. Click the cell for a student and period, type the score, and move to the next cell. You can tab through cells to enter grades quickly across the roster.

<Tip>
  Save your work frequently as you go. Unsaved changes are lost if you navigate away from the page.
</Tip>

### Grading periods

| Period | When to use |
|---|---|
| Prelim | First grading period of the term |
| Midterm | Mid-term examination or performance grades |
| Finals | End-of-term grades |

Each period is independent. You can enter and submit them at different times as the term progresses.

## Updating grades

You can edit any grade that has not yet been submitted for finalization. Open the **Grades** tab, click the cell you want to change, enter the new value, and save. Once a period has been submitted, contact your administrator to request a correction.

## Submitting grades for finalization

When you are satisfied with the grades for a period, submit them to begin the review process.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your entries">
    Scan the grades tab to confirm all students have scores entered for the period you are submitting. Empty cells will not be included in the submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the period to submit">
    On the **Grades** tab, choose the grading period (prelim, midterm, or finals) you want to finalize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit grades">
    Select **Submit grades** for that period. A confirmation prompt appears — confirm to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for administrator review">
    Your submission enters the review queue. You will be notified when the administrator has verified and posted the grades.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Submitted grades may not be edited until administrator verification is complete. Double-check all entries before submitting. If you need to correct a submitted grade, contact your administrator directly.
</Warning>

## Grade statistics

The portal displays summary statistics for each class once enough grades have been entered. You can view the class average, highest score, lowest score, and grade distribution from the **Grades** tab. These statistics update automatically as you enter or modify scores.

## When do students see their grades?

Grades become visible to students only after an administrator posts them. Even if you have submitted a grading period, students will not see scores until the posting step is complete.

<Note>
  Check with your administrator or registrar for the posting schedule at your institution. Posting timelines may vary by term or grading period.
</Note>
