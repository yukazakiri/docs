title: "Frequently asked questions"
description: "Answers to common questions about logging in, viewing grades, enrollment status, tuition assessments, 2FA setup, and managing your KoAkademy account."
---

This page answers the questions students and faculty ask most often. Topics include account access, grades, enrollment, tuition, and account settings. If you do not find what you are looking for here, you can submit a support ticket and the IT team will help you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password — how do I reset it?">
    On the login screen, click **Forgot your password?** below the sign-in form. Enter the email address linked to your account and click **Send reset link**. Check your inbox for an email from KoAkademy and follow the link to set a new password.

    If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. If you still do not receive it, contact your institution's IT helpdesk — your account email may need to be verified.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My grades are not showing — what do I do?">
    Grades appear in your portal only after your instructor has submitted and published them. If the submission deadline has passed and your grades are still missing, first check that you are viewing the correct semester using the semester selector in the top navigation.

    If the semester is correct and grades are still absent, contact your instructor to confirm they have been submitted. If the issue persists after the instructor confirms submission, submit a help ticket so the registrar's office can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot enroll — the form says enrollment is closed. Why?">
    Enrollment forms are only open during the enrollment period set by your school's administrator. Outside of that window, the form is unavailable to all students.

    Check your school's official announcements or the registrar's office for the published enrollment schedule. If you believe enrollment should currently be open, contact the registrar directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My tuition assessment looks wrong — what should I do?">
    Tuition assessments are generated based on your enrolled subjects and any applicable fees. If an amount looks incorrect, do not pay until the discrepancy is resolved.

    Take a screenshot of the assessment and bring it — or send the screenshot — to the cashier's office or the registrar. They can review the line items and issue a corrected assessment if needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I download my Statement of Account (SOA)?">
    Your Statement of Account is available in the **Finances** section of the student portal.

    1. Go to **Finances** in the sidebar.
    2. Locate the term you need the SOA for.
    3. Click **Download SOA** or the PDF icon next to that term.

    The PDF downloads to your device and includes your full payment history for the selected term.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?">
    Enabling 2FA adds a second verification step to your login, making your account more secure.

    1. Click your profile avatar or name in the top-right corner of the portal.
    2. Go to **Security** or **Account Settings**.
    3. Under **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Enable**.
    4. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator or Authy).
    5. Enter the six-digit code from the app to confirm setup.

    After enabling 2FA, save your recovery codes in a safe place. You will need them if you ever lose access to your authenticator app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I install KoAkademy on my phone?">
    KoAkademy is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can add it to your phone's home screen without downloading anything from an app store.

    **On Android (Chrome):**
    1. Open the portal in Chrome.
    2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
    3. Tap **Add to Home screen** and confirm.

    **On iPhone (Safari):**
    1. Open the portal in Safari.
    2. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

    The portal icon will appear on your home screen and open in a full-screen app experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am a faculty member but I cannot access the portal — what is wrong?">
    There are a few common reasons a faculty member may be unable to access the portal:

    - **Account not yet activated** — Your account may still be pending activation by the administrator. Contact the registrar or IT office to confirm your account status.
    - **Wrong URL** — Make sure you are using the faculty portal URL, not the student portal.
    - **Password issue** — Try resetting your password using the **Forgot your password?** link on the login screen.
    - **Access not assigned** — Your account may not yet be assigned to a school or organization. Contact the IT helpdesk to have your access configured.

    If none of these apply, submit a help ticket from another device or contact IT directly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change the semester I am viewing?">
    The semester selector is in the top navigation bar of the portal. Click it to see a dropdown of available school years and semesters.

    Select the term you want to view and the portal will reload your data — grades, schedule, finances — for that semester. Your selection is saved to your account, so it persists the next time you log in.

    <Note>
      Changing your personal semester view does not affect the institution's global active semester setting — it only changes what you see in your own account.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who do I contact for technical issues?">
    For issues you can resolve through the portal — such as missing grades, wrong tuition figures, or enrollment questions — use the built-in help ticket system at `/student/help` (students) or `/faculty/help` (faculty).

    For issues that prevent you from accessing the portal at all — such as a locked account or a password you cannot reset — contact your institution's IT helpdesk directly by phone or in person.

    You can find the support email and phone number on the login screen or in the portal footer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
