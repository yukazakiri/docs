---
title: "Submitting and tracking support tickets"
description: "Submit, reply to, and track support tickets in KoAkademy's built-in help system to get assistance from your institution's IT support team."
---

KoAkademy includes a built-in help ticket system so you can report issues and ask questions directly from the portal. Both students and faculty have access to the ticket system. Every ticket you submit is tracked, and support staff can reply to you within the same thread.

## How to submit a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the help section">
    - **Students**: go to `/student/help` in the portal navigation.
    - **Faculty**: go to `/faculty/help` in the portal navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new ticket">
    Click **New Ticket** (or **Submit a Request**, depending on your portal's label).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a clear **Subject** — for example, "Cannot view my grades for First Semester 2024–2025". Then describe your issue in the **Description** field. Include any relevant details such as the page you were on, what you tried, and what happened instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the ticket">
    Click **Submit**. You will see your new ticket appear in the ticket list with a status of **Open**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Include as much detail as possible — screenshots, the exact error message, and the steps you took before the issue occurred. More detail helps support staff resolve your ticket faster without needing to ask follow-up questions.
</Tip>

## Viewing your tickets

After submitting, you can return to the help section at any time to see all your tickets. Tickets are listed with their subject, submission date, and current status.

Click any ticket in the list to open the full conversation thread, including any replies from the support team.

## Replying to a ticket

If the support team asks for more information, or if you have an update to share, you can reply directly in the ticket thread.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Go to your help section and click the ticket you want to reply to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your reply">
    Scroll to the bottom of the thread and type your message in the reply box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your reply">
    Click **Send Reply**. Your message is added to the thread and the support team is notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ticket statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Open** | Your ticket has been submitted and is waiting for a response from the support team. |
| **In Progress** | A support staff member is actively working on your issue. |
| **Awaiting your reply** | The support team has responded and is waiting for more information from you. |
| **Resolved** | The issue has been addressed and the ticket is closed. |

<Note>
  Resolved tickets remain visible in your ticket history. If the same issue returns, you can submit a new ticket rather than reopening a closed one.
</Note>

## When to contact IT directly

The help ticket system is designed for non-urgent requests. For issues that prevent you from accessing the platform at all — such as a locked account or a forgotten password — contact your institution's IT helpdesk directly by phone or in person.

<Warning>
  If you are completely locked out of your account, you cannot access the ticket system. Use your institution's direct IT helpdesk contact for account recovery.
</Warning>
