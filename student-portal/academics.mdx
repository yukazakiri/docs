The academics section of the student portal gives you a complete view of your current coursework. You can see all the subjects you are enrolled in, check your weekly timetable, review grades posted by your faculty, and submit assignments — all in one place. The information shown depends on the semester and school year currently selected in the top navigation bar.

## My classes

Navigate to `/student/classes` to see a list of all subjects you are enrolled in for the current semester. Each entry shows:

- **Subject code** — the official code for the subject (e.g., IT101).
- **Description** — the full subject name.
- **Instructor** — the faculty member assigned to the subject.
- **Units** — the number of credit units the subject carries.
- **Room** — the room or venue where the class meets.

<Note>
  If your classes list is empty or does not match what you expect, check that the correct semester and school year are selected in the top navigation bar.
</Note>

## Class detail page

Click any subject in your classes list to open its detail page. Here you can:

- **Read announcements and posts** from your instructor, including course materials, reminders, and updates.
- **View and submit assignments** that your instructor has posted.

### Submitting an assignment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the class">
    Go to `/student/classes` and click the subject that has the assignment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the post">
    Scroll through the class posts to locate the assignment. Posts are listed in reverse chronological order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the assignment">
    Click the assignment post to view its instructions, deadline, and any attached files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your work">
    Click **Submit** and upload your file or enter your response, then confirm your submission. You will see a confirmation once it is received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule

Your weekly timetable is available at `/student/schedule`. It shows all your classes for the week laid out by day and time, including:

- Class subject and code.
- Start and end times.
- Room or venue.

Use the schedule view to plan your week and confirm class times without having to check each subject individually.

## Grades

Once your faculty posts grades for a grading period, they appear on your class detail page and in your overall grades view. Grades are organized by grading period:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Prelim">
    The preliminary grades reflect your performance during the first grading period of the semester.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Midterm">
    Midterm grades are posted at the midpoint of the semester.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Finals">
    Final grades are posted at the end of the semester and represent your overall performance for the term.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### GPA calculation

Your GPA for the semester is calculated automatically once all final grades are posted. The calculation is based on the grade values and unit weights of your enrolled subjects. If a subject has not yet received a final grade, it will not be included in the GPA calculation.

<Note>
  Grades are only visible after they have been officially posted by your instructor. If a grade is missing, your faculty member may not have submitted it yet. Contact your instructor or registrar if a grade remains missing after the end of the grading period.
</Note>

## Semester selector

All data in the academics section — classes, schedule, and grades — reflects the semester and school year you have selected in the top navigation bar. Switch the selector to view records from a previous term.
