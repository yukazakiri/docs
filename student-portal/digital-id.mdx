---

KoAkademy generates a digital student ID card from your registered student profile. You can access it any time from the portal, present it in full-screen mode when identification is required, and refresh it if your information has recently been updated. The digital ID card is always based on the data in your student record, so keeping your profile accurate ensures your card is correct.

## What your ID card shows

Your digital student ID card displays the following information:

- **Student name** — your full registered name.
- **Student ID number** — your unique identifier in the system.
- **Course** — the program you are enrolled in.
- **School year** — the current academic year associated with your enrollment.
- **Photo** — your profile photo as uploaded to your student record.

## Accessing your ID card

Navigate to `/student/id-card` to open your digital ID card. The card loads directly from your student profile, so no additional steps are needed to generate it.

## Viewing in full-screen presentation mode

When you need to present your ID — such as at a library, laboratory, or school event — use the full-screen presentation view for a cleaner display.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your ID card">
    Go to `/student/id-card` to load your digital ID card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to presentation mode">
    Click **View full screen** or navigate directly to `/student/id-card/view`. The card will expand to fill your screen with no navigation elements visible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present your ID">
    Show your screen to the staff member or guard who needs to verify your identity. Press **Escape** or use the back button to return to the normal view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refreshing your card data

If your student profile has been recently updated — for example, a new photo was uploaded or your course was changed — your ID card may not reflect the changes immediately. Use the refresh function to pull the latest data.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your ID card">
    Navigate to `/student/id-card`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the card">
    Click **Refresh card data**. The portal will fetch the latest information from your student record and update the displayed card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The card data comes directly from your registered student profile. If any information on the card is incorrect after refreshing, contact your registrar to have your profile updated at the source.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Save a screenshot of your ID card and store it in your phone's photo library. This gives you a copy to share or display when you do not have internet access and a physical card is not available.
</Tip>
