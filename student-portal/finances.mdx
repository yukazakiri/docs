The finances section gives you a transparent breakdown of what you owe and what you have already paid for the current semester. You can review every fee charged to your account, see the dates and reference numbers of past payments, and generate an official Statement of Account (SOA) whenever you need it. All amounts are displayed in the currency configured by your institution, which defaults to Philippine Peso (PHP).

## Tuition and fees

Go to `/student/tuition` to see a complete breakdown of the charges applied to your account for the current semester. The assessment typically includes:

- **Tuition fee** — the base charge for your enrolled units.
- **Laboratory fees** — additional charges for subjects that require lab access.
- **Miscellaneous fees** — other institutional fees such as library, ID, or development fees.

Each line item is listed separately so you can see exactly what makes up your total assessment.

<Note>
  The fees shown reflect the semester and school year currently selected in the top navigation bar. Switch the selector to review charges from a previous term.
</Note>

## Payment history

Below your fee assessment, the payment history table shows every payment recorded on your account. Each entry includes:

- **Date** — when the payment was posted.
- **Reference number** — the official reference for that transaction.
- **Amount** — how much was paid.

Use the reference number if you ever need to follow up on a payment with the cashier or your institution's finance office.

## Statement of Account (SOA)

Your SOA is an official document summarizing your fees and payments for the semester. You can generate and download it as a PDF at any time from `/student/tuition/soa`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the SOA page">
    Navigate to `/student/tuition/soa`. The portal will prepare your Statement of Account for the current semester.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the summary">
    Confirm that the semester, school year, and balance shown are correct before downloading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    Click **Download SOA** to save the PDF to your device. The file includes your student details, full fee breakdown, payment history, and remaining balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Download your SOA before the end of each semester and keep a copy for your records. You may need it for scholarship applications, employer reimbursements, or future enrollment requirements.
</Tip>

## Currency

All financial figures in the portal are displayed in your institution's configured currency. The default is Philippine Peso (PHP). If your institution uses a different currency, all amounts will be shown accordingly throughout the tuition and SOA pages.

<Warning>
  An outstanding balance may affect your ability to enroll for the next term. If you have unpaid fees, settle them with the cashier and confirm that the payment has been recorded in the portal before the next enrollment period opens.
</Warning>
