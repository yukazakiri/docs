The dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in to KoAkademy. It brings together your most important academic information in one place so you can quickly check what's coming up, confirm your enrollment status, and stay on top of any outstanding balances or announcements without navigating to separate pages.

## What the dashboard shows

When you open the dashboard, you'll see a summary of your current academic standing. The cards across the top display:

- **Enrollment status** — whether you are enrolled, an applicant, or have another status for the current term.
- **Current semester and school year** — the active period your data is filtered to.
- **Upcoming schedule** — your next classes for the day or week, pulled from your timetable.
- **Outstanding tuition balance** — the amount still owed for the current semester.
- **Recent announcements** — the latest posts from your institution.

## Switching semesters and school years

Your dashboard, classes, grades, and finances are all filtered to a specific semester and school year. To change which period you're viewing, use the settings selector in the top navigation bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the settings selector">
    Click the semester or school year label in the top navigation bar. A dropdown will appear showing available options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a semester">
    Choose the semester you want to view. The page will reload with data for that term.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a school year">
    Use the school year selector in the same area to switch between academic years if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Switching the semester or school year updates the context for the entire portal — your classes, grades, and tuition information will all reflect the selected period.
</Note>

## Switching active school

If your account is linked to more than one institution, you can switch between them without logging out. In the top navigation bar, click your current school's name or logo to open the school selector, then choose the institution you want to access. All portal data will reload for the selected school.

## Notifications panel

The notifications panel appears in the top navigation bar and shows alerts from your institution, such as enrollment reminders, payment confirmations, and announcements.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Marking a notification as read">
    Click the notification to open it. It will be marked as read automatically. To mark all notifications as read at once, click **Mark all as read** at the top of the notifications panel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Dismissing a notification">
    To remove a notification from your panel, click the dismiss (×) icon next to it. Dismissed notifications are permanently removed from your list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Global search

The search bar at the top of every page lets you find content across the portal — including classes, announcements, and pages — without knowing exactly where to navigate. Click the search icon or press the keyboard shortcut to open the search overlay, then type to filter results.
