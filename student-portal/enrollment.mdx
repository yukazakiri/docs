---
title: "How to enroll in KoAkademy"
description: "Complete your enrollment as a new or continuing student — from filling out the enrollment form and selecting subjects to tracking your application status."
---

Enrollment in KoAkademy depends on whether you are a first-time student or returning for another term. New students submit an enrollment application through a public form, while continuing students use a streamlined path that carries over their existing record. In both cases, enrollment is only available during the enrollment period set by your institution.

<Note>
  The enrollment form is only accessible while your institution has the enrollment period open. If you try to access it outside this window, you will see a message that enrollment is currently closed.
</Note>

## New student enrollment

If you are enrolling in KoAkademy for the first time, follow these steps to submit your application.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the enrollment page">
    Open your browser and navigate to `/enrollment`. You will see the new student enrollment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your personal information">
    Enter your full name (including middle name), date of birth, birthplace, civil status, religion, place of origin, contact details, and complete current address. Make sure all information matches your official documents. See [Required fields](#required-fields) for the full list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contacts, education, and PWD details">
    Provide at least one social media link, your father's and mother's contact numbers, a guardian, and your full education history (elementary, high school, and senior high school). Tick **Person with disability (PWD)** if applicable and select the type of disability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your course and strand">
    Choose your program, course, and strand (if applicable). The available options depend on what your institution offers — your registrar may also have curated a shorter list of courses that are open to new applicants this term.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your family income bracket">
    In the **Family Income** section, choose the option that best matches your family's approximate monthly income. This is optional but helps the registrar assess your eligibility for scholarships, financial aid, and other support programs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your application">
    Review your details and click **Submit**. Your application will be sent to the registrar for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Family income brackets

The **Monthly Income Bracket** dropdown lists ten ranges, from less than 10,000 up to more than 2,000,000 in your institution's configured currency. Currency symbols in each label are rendered automatically — for example, a Philippine peso institution shows `₱10,000 - ₱20,000`, while a US dollar institution shows `$10,000 - $20,000`.

| Value | Range |
| --- | --- |
| `below_10k` | Less than 10,000 |
| `10k_to_20k` | 10,000 – 20,000 |
| `20k_to_40k` | 20,000 – 40,000 |
| `40k_to_80k` | 40,000 – 80,000 |
| `80k_to_150k` | 80,000 – 150,000 |
| `150k_to_250k` | 150,000 – 250,000 |
| `250k_to_500k` | 250,000 – 500,000 |
| `500k_to_1m` | 500,000 – 1,000,000 |
| `1m_to_2m` | 1,000,000 – 2,000,000 |
| `above_2m` | More than 2,000,000 |

<Note>
  Your selection is saved on your student record and is visible only to your registrar and authorized staff. Leave the field as **Select income range...** if you prefer not to disclose this information.
</Note>

### What happens after you submit

After submitting, your enrollment moves through several stages before it is confirmed.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pending">
    Your application has been received and is waiting for an administrator to review it. You do not need to do anything at this stage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin review">
    The registrar or enrollment officer is reviewing your submitted information. They may contact you if additional documents are required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Confirmed">
    Your enrollment has been approved. You will receive a notification and your status will update to **Enrolled** in the portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Required fields

The new student enrollment form is split into steps. Each step validates before you can continue, so any missing or malformed entry highlights the field with an error message. Use the tables below to see which fields are required at each step and what format the form expects.

### Personal information

Provided on the **Personal information** step of the form.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First name | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| Middle name | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. Enter your full middle name — initials are not accepted. |
| Last name | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| Birth date | Yes | Use the date picker. |
| Birthplace | Yes | Enter as `City, Province`. |
| Gender | Yes | Select from the dropdown. |
| Civil status | Yes | Single, Married, Widowed, Separated, or Divorced. |
| Religion | Yes | Free text, for example `Roman Catholic`. |
| Nationality | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| City of origin | Yes | Your hometown city. |
| Province of origin | Yes | Province of your hometown. |
| Region of origin | Yes | Region of your hometown. |
| Email address | Yes | Must be a valid email — the form rejects malformed addresses. |
| Phone number | Yes | Digits only, at least 10 characters. |
| Complete current address | Yes | At least 5 characters. Enter as `House No., Street, Barangay, City, Province`. This value is also saved as your address on file. |
| Permanent address | Optional | Enter only if different from your current address. |

<Note>
  Email address and phone number are now required for every applicant. Previously, email could be left blank — make sure you have a working email since the registrar uses it to notify you about your application status.
</Note>

### Contacts, family, and education

Provided on the **Contacts** step of the form.

| Field | Required | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn) | At least one | You must provide a link or handle for at least one of the four. Leave the others blank if you do not use them. |
| Emergency contact name | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| Emergency contact phone | Yes | Digits only. |
| Father's contact number | Yes | Digits only, at least 10 characters. |
| Mother's contact number | Yes | Digits only, at least 10 characters. |
| Guardian name | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| Guardian relationship | Yes | Letters, spaces, periods, and hyphens only. |
| Guardian contact number | Yes | Digits only. |
| Elementary school and year graduated | Yes | School name and a 4-digit year. |
| High school and year graduated | Yes | School name and a 4-digit year. |
| Senior high school and year graduated | Yes | School name and a 4-digit year. |
| College and vocational fields | Optional | Fill in only if you attended college or vocational school before enrolling. |

<Tip>
  If your father, mother, or guardian shares a phone number, enter the same number in each field. The form does not check that the three contacts are unique — it only requires that each has a valid number.
</Tip>

### Person with disability (PWD)

The **Contacts** step includes a **Person with disability (PWD)** checkbox.

- Leave the checkbox **unchecked** if it does not apply to you. No further action is needed.
- Tick the checkbox to declare PWD status. The **Type of disability** field becomes required — for example, `Visual impairment` or `Hearing impairment`. The form will not let you continue until you describe the disability type.

<Note>
  PWD information is stored on your student record and is visible only to your registrar and authorized staff. It is used to coordinate accommodations and support services.
</Note>

## Continuing student enrollment

If you are already registered in KoAkademy and are enrolling for a new term, use the continuing enrollment path on the same `/enrollment` page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the enrollment page">
    Navigate to `/enrollment` and select the **Continuing student** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look up your record">
    Enter your student ID or registered email address to retrieve your existing student record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your details">
    Verify that your personal information and course details are correct. Update anything that has changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your subjects">
    Choose the subjects you want to enroll in for the current term. Available subjects are loaded automatically based on your course and year level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Submit your continuing enrollment. Your request will go through the same review process as a new application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Family income

The enrollment form collects family income details so the institution can assess eligibility for scholarships and support programs. You control how income is reported using two settings.

### Income basis

Use the **Income Basis** dropdown to choose how your family reports income:

- **Annual Income** — Report total household income per year. This is the default.
- **Monthly Income** — Report total household income per month.

The income range options below the dropdown update automatically based on your selection. If you change the basis after picking a range, your previous selection is cleared so you can choose an appropriate range for the new basis.

### Same or separate parent income

Below the income basis, the checkbox **Father and mother have the same income bracket** controls how many ranges you submit:

- **Checked (default)** — Select one **Family Income Bracket** that applies to both parents combined.
- **Unchecked** — Select a separate **Father's Income Bracket** and **Mother's Income Bracket**. Use this when each parent earns within a different range.

<Tip>
  If only one parent is earning, leave the checkbox unchecked and select the appropriate range for that parent. Choose the lowest range for the non-earning parent.
</Tip>

### Income range examples

The available ranges depend on the basis you select. The table below shows representative ranges; the exact options shown in the form follow your institution's currency and configuration.

| Income basis | Example ranges |
|---|---|
| **Annual** | ₱250,000 and below · ₱250,001 – ₱400,000 · ₱400,001 – ₱800,000 · ₱800,001 – ₱2,000,000 · ₱2,000,001 – ₱8,000,000 · Above ₱8,000,000 |
| **Monthly** | Less than ₱10,000 · ₱10,000 – ₱20,000 · ₱20,000 – ₱40,000 · ₱40,000 – ₱80,000 · ... · More than ₱2,000,000 |

<Note>
  When the same-bracket checkbox is unchecked, both **Father's Income Bracket** and **Mother's Income Bracket** are required. When it is checked, only the shared **Family Income Bracket** is required.
</Note>

## Subject selection

When you reach the subject selection step, the portal loads only the subjects that apply to your course and year level. You will not see subjects outside your curriculum. If a subject you expect to see is missing, contact your registrar — it may need to be added to your curriculum by an administrator.

## Family income

The enrollment form asks for your family's income range so the institution can assess eligibility for scholarships, discounts, and support programs. You answer in three small steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an income basis">
    Choose **Annual Income** or **Monthly Income** under **Income Basis**. The bracket options below this field update to match the basis you select. If you change the basis later, any bracket you already selected is cleared so you can pick a fresh range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to enter one or two brackets">
    Select **Father and mother have the same income bracket** if both parents earn within the same range. Clear the checkbox if their incomes fall into different ranges so you can record each parent separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the bracket(s)">
    With the checkbox selected, pick a single **Family Income Bracket**. With the checkbox cleared, pick a **Father's Income Bracket** and a **Mother's Income Bracket**. All bracket options come from the same income basis you chose in step 1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The default income basis is **Annual Income**. Your institution can change the default and customize the available brackets in `config/income_brackets.php`. See [academic settings](/configuration/academic-settings) if you administer the portal.
</Note>

### Income bracket fields

| Field | When it is required |
| --- | --- |
| **Income Basis** | Always required. Determines which brackets you can pick from. |
| **Family Income Bracket** | Required when **Father and mother have the same income bracket** is selected. |
| **Father's Income Bracket** | Required when the same-bracket checkbox is cleared. |
| **Mother's Income Bracket** | Required when the same-bracket checkbox is cleared. |

<Tip>
  If you are unsure which bracket to choose, pick the range closest to your family's typical earnings. The registrar can adjust the value later if your records show otherwise.
</Tip>

## Enrollment statuses

After you submit your application, your account is assigned an enrollment status. The table below describes what each status means.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Applicant** | Your enrollment application has been submitted and is under review. |
| **Enrolled** | Your enrollment has been confirmed for the current term. |
| **Withdrawn** | You have voluntarily withdrawn from enrollment before the term began. |
| **Dropped** | You were removed from enrollment after the term started, either voluntarily or by the institution. |
| **Graduate** | You have completed your program and graduated. |

<Warning>
  Outstanding balances or incomplete requirements may prevent your enrollment from being confirmed, even if you have submitted the form. Check with your institution's registrar if your status remains as **Applicant** longer than expected.
</Warning>
