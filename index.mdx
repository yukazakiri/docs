## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the portal">
    Navigate to your institution's KoAkademy portal URL (e.g., `portal.koakademy.edu`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Use the school email and password provided by your registrar. Change your password after your first login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your dashboard">
    The dashboard shows your active enrollment, upcoming schedule, and any outstanding tuition balance at a glance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your semester">
    Use the semester selector in the top navigation to make sure you are viewing the correct term's data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are an administrator setting up KoAkademy for your institution, start with [Branding & Site Settings](/configuration/branding) to configure your school's name, logo, and theme.
</Note>
