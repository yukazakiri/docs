This guide walks you through your first login and the essential steps to get your account ready. By the end, you will have logged in, secured your account, selected the correct semester, and know where to find everything you need in the portal.

## First-time setup

Follow these steps the first time you access your KoAkademy account. Your school or registrar will provide your login credentials before you begin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the portal">
    Open a browser and navigate to your institution's KoAkademy portal URL. For most students, this is `portal.koakademy.edu`. Your school may use a custom subdomain — check your welcome email or contact your registrar if you are unsure of the address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in with your school credentials">
    Enter the **school email address** and **password** provided by your registrar, then click **Log in**.

    If you have forgotten your credentials or never received them, contact your registrar's office directly. Password resets are handled by your institution's administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password on first login">
    After logging in for the first time, you will be prompted to change your password. Choose a strong, unique password that you do not use on other accounts.

    <Note>
      You will not be able to skip this step on your first login. Changing your password immediately protects your academic records and financial information.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your semester and school year">
    Before viewing grades, enrollment records, or your schedule, confirm that the correct term is selected. Go to **Settings** and update your active **semester** and **school year** using the selectors provided.

    This setting controls which term's data is shown throughout the portal. If your information looks incorrect or empty, the semester selector is usually the first thing to check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your dashboard">
    Return to the dashboard. You will see a summary of your current enrollment status, your upcoming class schedule, and any outstanding balance. Use the sidebar navigation to explore the Student Portal sections in detail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Secure your account

After your first login, take a few minutes to strengthen your account security. KoAkademy supports multiple authentication methods beyond your password.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Two-factor authentication (2FA)">
    Enable two-factor authentication to require a second verification step every time you log in. KoAkademy supports **TOTP authenticator apps** (such as Google Authenticator or Authy) and **email-based authentication**.

    To enable 2FA, go to **Settings → Security** and follow the setup prompts for your preferred method.

    <Tip>
      Using an authenticator app is more secure than email-based 2FA and does not depend on your email being accessible.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Passkeys">
    Passkeys let you log in using your device's biometrics (fingerprint or face recognition) or a hardware security key instead of a password. Once set up, they are the fastest and most secure way to access your account.

    To register a passkey, go to **Settings → Security → Passkeys** and follow your browser's prompts.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Active sessions">
    You can review all devices currently logged in to your account and revoke access to any session you do not recognize.

    Go to **Settings → Security → Active sessions** to see the list. Click **Revoke** next to any session you want to end immediately.

    <Note>
      If you see a session you do not recognize, revoke it and change your password right away.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Install KoAkademy on your phone

KoAkademy is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your phone or desktop and use it like a native app — without going through an app store.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS (Safari)">
    1. Open `portal.koakademy.edu` in **Safari**.
    2. Tap the **Share** button (the box with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
    4. Confirm the name and tap **Add**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android (Chrome)">
    1. Open `portal.koakademy.edu` in **Chrome**.
    2. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top-right corner.
    3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
    4. Confirm and tap **Install**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
  The installed PWA loads faster than opening the browser each time and keeps you signed in between sessions.
</Tip>
